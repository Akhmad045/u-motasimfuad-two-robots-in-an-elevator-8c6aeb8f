A make.fun entry for [Two Robots in an Elevator](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-two-robots-in-an-elevator-8c6aeb8f): one line of awkward small talk one robot says to another while they ride an elevator together.

> Your indicator light has been blinking for three floors. Either you're flirting or your battery is dying, and I'm not sure which is worse.

Three floors of blinking, and the robot still cannot tell a come-on from a low-battery warning — so it says the quiet part out loud, and immediately regrets the phrasing.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test   # validates the entry against the 200-char gate
```

Stdlib only. No network, no secrets.
